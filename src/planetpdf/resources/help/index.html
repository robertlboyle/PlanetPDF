Welcome to PlanetPDF

PlanetPDF opens, reads, edits, fills in, signs, protects, and prints PDF files. Its menus, tools, and keyboard shortcuts follow Adobe Acrobat's, so if you have used Acrobat you'll find most things where you expect them.

Using this help

Press F1 or choose Help ▸ PlanetPDF Help to open this manual. On the left:

  • Contents: every chapter and its sections, in order.
  • Index: type a word to find the sections about it.
  • Search: finds every section with all the words you type.

The PDF button at the top right opens the whole manual as a PDF, to print or keep. The same file, PlanetPDF Manual.pdf, is in the folder PlanetPDF is installed in.

What's in this manual

  • Getting Started: installing, the window, opening and saving files.
  • Reading and Finding: moving around, zooming, finding words, reading aloud.
  • Organizing Pages: moving, adding, removing, and turning pages; headers, watermarks, Bates numbers, bookmarks.
  • Editing Text and Pictures: changing a PDF's own text, pictures, shapes, and links.
  • Comments, Notes, and Stamps: marking up a document and discussing it.
  • Forms: Filling In and Making: filling in forms and making your own.
  • Signatures: your signature and initials, and digital signatures.
  • Protecting and Redacting: passwords, certificates, removing information for good.
  • Scans, Text Recognition, and File Size: scanning, making scans searchable, cleaning them up, making files smaller.
  • Creating, Converting, and Comparing: making PDFs, saving them as other kinds of files, PDF/A, comparing versions.
  • Printing and the PlanetPDF Printer: printing, and printing any program's documents into a PDF.
  • Scripts, Programs, and Plugins: using PlanetPDF from scripts and other programs.
  • Preferences: every setting.
  • Menu Reference and Keyboard Shortcuts: every command.
  • Troubleshooting: what to do when something doesn't work.
  • What's New: the changes in each version.
