Our principles

Planet Networks builds these tools to use ourselves, and we're happy to share them with our customers and the public. Every program on this site is made the same way.

In short Free to use, free to modify and improve, open for anyone to examine, and it never collects information about you. No ads, no spyware, no adware.

Why it's free

Planet Networks' business is providing amazing Internet, phone, and TV services, plus connectivity services for businesses. Software is something that helps everyone, and it costs us very little to make, so we share it. If you live or work in our coverage area, we hope you'll use our services: see planet.net.

Free to use, modify, and improve

The software costs nothing, and you may change it and make it better. There is nothing to buy, no subscription, and no trial period.

Open for anyone to examine

We want you to be able to see what our programs do and how they do it. Anyone can read the code to make sure a program does only what we intend and what we say it does. (The source code is being prepared for publication; a link will appear on each program's page.)

Your information stays yours

Our programs never collect information about you or how you use them. We never share any information with Planet Networks, advertisers, or anyone else. There are no advertisements, and nothing else is ever bundled with our software: no spyware and no adware.

A program only goes online when you use something that needs it. PlanetPDF, for example, checks this site for updates (you can turn that off in Preferences) and downloads the optional natural reading voices when you ask for them. Those requests carry nothing about you or your documents; like any web request, our server sees your computer's internet address.

When you report a problem

Send feedback and bug reports by email to software@planet.net. If you tell us about a bug or a problem, you decide what to send. What you send us may include some information about you or your files; we use it only to look into the problem you reported. We never see anything you don't send us yourself.

Everywhere you work

We build each program to run on Windows, Mac, and Linux, and on Android and iPhone or iPad where we can, from one shared design, so it works the same wherever you use it.

No warranty, no support

The programs are provided as is. We intend to fix reported bugs quickly and to add good suggestions and improvements that make sense, but there is no promise of this. A commercial support contract for businesses may come later; it isn't offered today. See the freeware terms.
